Safety reminders

For Safety Reasons, the DVD system of this unit will only
 show display if the handbrake is engaged.

Manufacturer will not be held liable for any accident 
 caused if this safety feature is bypassed, or tampered. 

Tampering and/or any modification done on the radio in 
 order to bypass this safety feature are strictly 
 prohibited and will void warranty.
 
 
The Navi system is only a tool to help the driver navigate
 through general areas. 

It is designed to provide route place driver's attentiveness
 and good judgement. 
 
In any case, do not follow navi instructions if they
suggest an unsafe or illegal maneuver or would place the
vehicle in an unsafe condition.

When using the device, carefully compare information
 displayed on the device to all visibly available navigation
 sources such as traffic signs, road closures, road
 conditions,traffic congestions, weather conditions and
 other factors that may affect safety during driving. 

For safety purposes, always resolve any discrepancies
 between actual condition and display on the monitor
 before continuing navigation.


Minimize the amount of time spent viewing the LCD
 screen while driving and use voice prompts instead.

Do not be distracted by the LCD screen.Do not input 
 destination,change settings or access any function
 requiring prolonged focus on the LCD while driving. 

It is advised to park the vehicle in a safe area before 
 attempting such operations.
