Caution

The Navi system is only a tool to help the driver navigate
through general areas. 
It is designed to provide route suggestions only and is 
not meant to replace driver's attentiveness and good 
judgement. 
In any case, do not follow navi instructions if they 
suggest an unsafe or illegal maneuver or would place the 
vehicle in an unsafe condition.
For complete list of other safety reminders, please go to 
Safety Info.
